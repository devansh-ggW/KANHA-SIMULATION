01

Why Kanha?

KANHA SIMULATION uses Krishna-inspired imagery as the presentation layer for science. The visual universe is expressive; the underlying relationships remain physical models.

02

Why separate pages?

Each experiment lives at its own URL and loads only its shared shell plus the simulation engine. That keeps navigation clean and reduces unnecessary work for classroom computers.

03

Why lightweight?

No backend, no heavy 3D engine and no permanent decorative render loop. Canvas is used only for the live experiment, and it stops drawing when paused.
